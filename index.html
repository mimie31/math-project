🌴 Malaysian Oil Palm Growth Derivative Lab

Scenario: You are monitoring an oil palm plantation in Perak. We model the trunk height using a simplified Gompertz-style logistic function. In bioscience, the 1st derivative f'(x) represents the instantaneous growth rate, helping us predict when the tree will reach a height that requires specialized harvesting equipment.

Height Function: f(x) = L / (1 + e-k(x - x₀))
Growth Rate (Derivative): f'(x) = k · f(x) · (1 - f(x)/L)
Where: L=15m (Max height), k=0.25 (Growth constant), x₀=8 (Midpoint year)
