🌱 Bio-Calculus: Oil Palm Seedling Velocity

Program Context: SFA / SFM / SFP

In Malaysia, the growth of Elaeis guineensis (Oil Palm) seedlings follows a sigmoidal pattern. As a bioscience expert, we model the height $H$ over time $t$ (weeks) using the logistic function. To optimize nursery management, we calculate the derivative to find the growth rate.

Growth Function:
$$f(x) = \frac{L}{1 + e^{-k(x - x_0)}}$$
Where L=150cm (max height), k=0.2 (growth rate constant), x₀=20 (mid-point).
The Derivative (Instantaneous Growth Rate):
$$f'(x) = \frac{L \cdot k \cdot e^{-k(x - x_0)}}{(1 + e^{-k(x - x_0)})^2}$$
